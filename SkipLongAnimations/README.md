# Skip Long Animations
Cuts animations which take longer than they need to, so you can get on with your day.

**[Download the latest version](https://www.nexusmods.com/stardewvalley/mods/54146)**, then unzip it into your game's `Mods` folder. It needs [SMAPI](https://smapi.io).

- **Pam's bus:** when you take the bus to or from the Calico Desert, you get on as soon as you buy the ticket, instead of walking to the door. Once the door shuts, the screen fades and the bus is already parked at the other end, opening its door for you. The ticket price, Pam needing to be at work, and the reminder about your horse waiting at the bus stop are all as in the base game.
- **Clint's geodes:** Clint cracks geodes, artifact troves, Golden Coconuts and mystery boxes 5× faster. You still see the hammer, the crack and what was inside.

## Settings
Edit `config.json` in the mod's folder, or use [Generic Mod Config Menu](https://www.nexusmods.com/stardewvalley/mods/5098) in game.

| Setting | Default | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Skip the bus ride (`SkipBusRide`) | on | Skip the walk to the bus and the drive. Off, the bus ride plays as in the base game. |
| Geode cracking speed (`GeodeSpeed`) | 5 | How many times faster Clint cracks geodes, from 1 (the base game's speed) to 10. |

In multiplayer, each player can install it or not; it only changes what you see.
